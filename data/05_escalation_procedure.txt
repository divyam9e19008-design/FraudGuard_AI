Document: Escalation and Investigation Procedure
Department: Fraud Prevention & Risk
Applies to: All flagged account-opening applications

1. Purpose
This procedure describes the workflow an investigator should follow once an application is flagged by the automated risk-scoring system.

2. Risk bands
2.1 LOW (score below 30): No manual action required. Application proceeds through standard processing.
2.2 MEDIUM (score 30–69): Application is queued for a single-reviewer check. Reviewer confirms whether any single strong signal (no address history, unverifiable phone, top-decile credit request) is present without corroborating signals; if so, application may be cleared with a note.
2.3 HIGH (score 70 and above): Application is queued for two-reviewer sign-off. Both reviewers must independently confirm the risk factors before a decision is finalized.

3. Investigation steps
3.1 Reviewer examines the top contributing factors surfaced by the risk-scoring system for the specific application (not just the overall model-wide feature importance).
3.2 Reviewer cross-checks each surfaced factor against the relevant policy document (Identity Verification, Device and Session Risk, Income-Based Risk Tiering, or Contact Verification) to determine whether the factor is, on its own, a known weak or strong signal.
3.3 Reviewer documents which combination of factors drove the decision, for audit purposes.

4. Decision threshold selection
4.1 The operating decision threshold (the cutoff at which the model flags an application as requiring review) should be selected based on the current cost of a missed fraud versus the cost of an unnecessary manual review, not fixed permanently at a single value.
4.2 A lower threshold catches more fraud but increases the manual review workload; a higher threshold reduces reviewer workload but risks missing more fraud. This trade-off should be revisited whenever fraud losses or review capacity change materially.

5. Escalation contact
Cases meeting HIGH band criteria with two-reviewer disagreement should be escalated to the Fraud Prevention team lead for final determination.
