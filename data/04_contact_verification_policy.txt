Document: Contact Verification Policy
Department: Fraud Prevention & Risk
Applies to: New account-opening applications

1. Purpose
This policy governs how home phone verification status and free-email-domain usage should be incorporated into risk scoring.

2. Background
Phone-verification status was identified as one of the top contributing features in the fraud detection model, ranking alongside income and device signals in overall importance. Free-email-domain usage (as opposed to a paid or employer-issued domain) also showed a measurable, if smaller, association with fraud outcomes.

3. Policy
3.1 An application where the home phone number cannot be verified must be flagged for enhanced review if it co-occurs with any other elevated signal (no address history, device anomaly, top-decile credit limit request).
3.2 Free-email-domain usage alone is common among legitimate applicants and must not trigger review in isolation.
3.3 Applications with verified phone AND an established address history should generally be treated as lower priority for manual review, all other signals being neutral.

4. Escalation threshold
Two or more unverifiable contact signals occurring together (phone unverifiable, email domain flagged, or both) should be escalated per the Escalation Procedure document, regardless of the overall computed risk score.
