What this is
Two paragraphs, no adjectives.

A fraud-detection system for bank account-opening applications. A Random Forest scores each application on 46 features; the score becomes a 0–100 risk number with contributing factors. Those factors become a policy question, answered by a hybrid retriever — BM25 for exact terms, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 for meaning — whose top three sections are the only material the language model may use. If they do not answer it, it returns NOT_COVERED.

The hard part was never accuracy. At a 1.1% fraud rate, a model that labels everything safe scores 99% and catches nothing. Class weighting, SMOTE, threshold tuning, ranking instead of banding — every decision here exists to work around that one fact, and each is recorded with what it cost.

02
Retrieval & generation
PHASE 2

Six documents chunked on numbered headings, dual-indexed, min–max normalised and blended before the top three reach the model.

  • rank-bm25 0.2.2
  • sentence-transformers 6.0.1
  • torch 2.14.0
  • groq / gpt-oss-120b temp 0.2
No vector database. At 31 chunks, a 31 × 384 numpy array plus an in-memory BM25 index beats any server round-trip — the similarity is one matrix multiply. A vector store earns its place above roughly 10⁵ chunks; adding one here would be architecture for the résumé.

What comes next
Ordered by how much each changes what the system can honestly claim.
01 · EVALUATION
Record the deployed model’s metrics

Re-run evaluation for RF + SMOTE and write down precision, recall, F1 and PR-AUC — not ROC-AUC, which flatters a classifier at a 1.1% base rate.

02 · CORRECTNESS
Fix the two bugs

Replace val > 0 with a per-feature direction map, and re-select the threshold on a validation split.

03 · EXPLAINABILITY
Per-case SHAP attribution

Signed per-application contributions — the only way to satisfy 05_escalation_procedure §3.1, which the pipeline currently violates.

04 · RETRIEVAL
Widen, then rerank

Raise top_k, add a cross-encoder reranker, and split multi-factor queries into one retrieval each.

05 · CALIBRATION
Make the score mean something

Probability calibration, or replace fixed bands with population percentile. At a 0.20 ceiling, HIGH is unreachable.

06 · VALIDATION
Split by time, not at random

A temporal split is the honest test of whether the model generalises past its training period.

The knowledge-graph layer stays scoped out until there is a question it answers better than the current retriever — relationship features over shared devices and emails would be the reason to build one, not the diagram.
Data provenance
What is real, and what was written for demonstration.
📊
The dataset is real

Bank Account Fraud (BAF), NeurIPS 2022 — a published benchmark for fraud detection under realistic class imbalance.

This project uses Base.csv with target fraud_bool. These are account-opening applications, not card transactions — there is no amount, merchant, currency or location in the data, and nothing on this site invents one.

sgpjesus/bank-account-fraud-dataset-neurips-20221,000,000 rows~1.1% fraud
⚠
The policy corpus is not

All six policy documents were written for this project. Every file opens with [ILLUSTRATIVE INTERNAL POLICY — FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY], and that line sits in the indexed text, not just the header.

They are not real bank policy and must not be read as guidance. They were authored to be internally consistent and to cite this project’s own measured findings — the 4.5× address-history figure, the 0.58% → 2.16% income gradient — so retrieval has something honest to ground against.
